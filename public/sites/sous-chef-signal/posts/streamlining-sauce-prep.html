Saucy shortcuts: 5 ways to prep ahead

Prep sauces like a pro

Takeaway

Prepping sauces ahead can save home cooks up to 30 minutes on weeknights

What happened

Many home cooks struggle to balance sauce prep with cooking weeknight dinners. Recently, several popular cooking blogs have highlighted the benefits of prepping sauces in advance. This trend has led to a surge in creative sauce prep methods and tools.

Why it matters

Prepping sauces ahead can be a game-changer for home cooks, allowing for more free time and reducing stress during weeknights. It also enables cooks to focus on other aspects of meal prep, such as cooking proteins and vegetables. By streamlining sauce prep, cooks can create a more efficient and enjoyable cooking experience.

How to think about it

To start prepping sauces ahead, consider investing in a few key tools, such as a blender or food processor, and designate a specific area of your pantry for sauce ingredients. Plan your meals for the week, and prep sauces that can be used in multiple dishes. Finally, experiment with different sauce recipes to find the ones that work best for you and your family.

Pros
  • Saves time
  • Reduces stress
  • Increases flexibility
Cons
  • Requires planning
  • Takes up pantry space
  • Can be messy
Watch out

Be cautious not to over-prep sauces, as they can spoil if not stored properly

FAQ

How long can I store prepped sauces?

Most prepped sauces can be stored in the fridge for up to 5 days or frozen for up to 3 months

What are some essential sauce ingredients to keep on hand?

Olive oil, soy sauce, and tomato paste are great staples to have in your pantry

Can I prep sauces in bulk?

Yes, prepping sauces in bulk can be a great way to save time and money, just be sure to follow safe food storage guidelines
