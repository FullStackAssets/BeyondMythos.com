Batch-prep your go-to sauce base in 30 minutes

Create a versatile tomato-onion base in half an hour and keep weeknight meals humming.

Takeaway

A ready-made sauce base cuts cooking time and boosts flavor consistency.

What happened

I spent a Friday night simmering onions, garlic, canned tomatoes, and herbs until they formed a thick, aromatic puree. The batch filled three jars, each lasting a week in the fridge.

Why it matters

Weeknight dinners often stall at the sauce step, leading to takeout or bland meals. Having a pre‑made base eliminates that bottleneck and ensures every dish starts with depth.

How to think about it

Treat the sauce base as a template: adjust spice levels, add protein or veg, and you have a new dinner in minutes. Keep it in the fridge or freezer for flexibility.

Pros
  • Saves 15‑20 min per meal
  • Consistent flavor profile
  • Freezes well for future weeks
Cons
  • Initial 30‑min prep
  • Requires pantry staples
  • Jar space needed
Watch out

Cool the sauce completely before sealing to avoid condensation and spoilage.

FAQ

Can I use fresh tomatoes instead of canned?

Yes, blanch, peel, and crush them; the cooking time may increase slightly.

How long does the base keep in the fridge?

Up to 5 days refrigerated; 3 months frozen.

What herbs work best for a neutral base?

Basil, oregano, and a pinch of thyme give a versatile flavor without dominating.
