Spice up your week: a 30-minute macro-balanced chili

A fast, protein-rich chili that hits your macro goals without the kitchen marathon.

Takeaway

You can hit protein, carbs, and fat targets in 30 minutes with pantry staples.

What happened

I tossed lean ground turkey, canned beans, and diced tomatoes into a pot, spiced it with cumin and smoked paprika, and let it simmer while I crushed a quick batch of quinoa. The result was a fragrant, balanced bowl ready to portion for the week.

Why it matters

Busy pros need meals that fuel performance and keep the fridge tidy. A macro-balanced chili reduces guesswork and prevents macro drift across the week.

How to think about it

Start by calculating your target protein per meal, then choose a lean protein source and pair it with fiber-rich beans and a modest carb side. Season aggressively—flavor compensates for the speed.

Pros
  • High protein per serving
  • Ready in 30 min
  • Freezer‑friendly portions
Cons
  • Can be spicy for some
  • Requires quinoa prep
  • Limited veg variety
Watch out

Watch the simmer to avoid a burnt bottom.

FAQ

Can I swap turkey for beef?

Yes, just adjust cooking time and keep the lean‑to‑fat ratio around 80/20.

How long will it keep in the freezer?

Up to three months when stored in airtight containers.

What if I’m low‑carb?

Replace quinoa with cauliflower rice or omit it entirely.
