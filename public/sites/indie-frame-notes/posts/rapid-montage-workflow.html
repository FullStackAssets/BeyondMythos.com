Rapid montage workflow: edit faster with these shortcuts

Cut your edit time in half with a handful of quick‑cut tricks that fit indie budgets.

Takeaway

Smart shortcuts can shave hours off your post‑production without sacrificing quality.

What happened

We tested a five‑step montage system on a 10‑minute short film, using only free or built‑in tools in DaVinci Resolve. The team logged time, compared output, and noted workflow hiccups.

Why it matters

Indie creators often juggle tight schedules and limited resources, so any time‑saving method directly impacts festival deadlines and budget overruns. Faster edits also free creative bandwidth for polishing story beats.

How to think about it

Treat each cut as a micro‑task: organize bins, set in‑out points, apply a preset transition, and move on. Batch similar shots, use keyboard macros, and lock your timeline before the final polish.

Pros
  • Cuts time dramatically
  • Works with free software
  • Easy to teach teammates
Cons
  • May oversimplify complex scenes
  • Relies on consistent footage format
  • Initial setup takes a few minutes
Watch out

Don’t let speed sacrifice narrative rhythm—always review the flow before exporting.

FAQ

Can this workflow be used in Premiere Pro?

Yes, the same principles apply; just map the shortcuts to Premiere’s keyboard settings.

What if my footage is mixed frame rates?

Standardize to a single frame rate in the prep stage to avoid sync issues during batch cuts.

Is there a risk of losing metadata?

If you use proxy files, ensure you relink before the final render to preserve original metadata.
