Paddling in the Dark: A Beginner's Guide

Kayaking at night requires prep

Takeaway

Paddling at night can be safe and enjoyable with proper planning and equipment

What happened

Many paddlers are hesitant to venture out at night, but with the right gear and mindset, it can be a unique and exhilarating experience. A recent trip on a calm lake under a full moon highlighted the importance of proper lighting and navigation. The darkness can be intimidating, but it also amplifies the senses and creates a sense of adventure

Why it matters

Paddling at night allows kayakers to experience the water in a new way, with different sounds, sights, and sensations. It can also be a great way to avoid crowds and paddle during cooler temperatures. Additionally, learning to navigate in the dark can improve overall paddling skills and confidence

How to think about it

To paddle safely at night, consider the moon phase, weather, and water conditions before heading out. Invest in a good light source, such as a headlamp or deck light, and bring a spare battery. It's also essential to file a float plan and let someone know your expected return time

Pros
  • Increased sense of adventure
  • Improved navigation skills
  • Cooler temperatures
Cons
  • Reduced visibility
  • Increased risk of collisions
  • Cold water hazards
Watch out

Be cautious of underwater obstacles and other watercraft when paddling at night

FAQ

What type of light is best for paddling at night?

A headlamp or deck light with a red light mode to preserve night vision

How can I stay warm while paddling at night?

Dress in layers, wear a wetsuit or dry suit, and bring a warm hat and gloves

What should I do if I get lost while paddling at night?

Stay calm, use your light to signal for help, and try to find a landmark or familiar spot
