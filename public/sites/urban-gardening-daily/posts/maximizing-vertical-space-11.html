Boosting yields with trellises and arbors

Get more from your balcony

Takeaway

Using trellises and arbors can significantly increase your container garden's yield

What happened

Apartment dwellers often face the challenge of limited space for their gardens. Recent innovations in trellis and arbor designs have made it possible to maximize vertical space. By utilizing these structures, gardeners can train climbing plants to grow upwards, thereby increasing their overall yield.

Why it matters

For balcony gardeners, making the most of available space is crucial. With the help of trellises and arbors, gardeners can not only grow more plants but also add aesthetic value to their outdoor space. This can lead to a more sustainable and enjoyable gardening experience.

How to think about it

When selecting a trellis or arbor, consider the type of plant you want to grow and the amount of support it will need. Choose a structure that is sturdy and easy to assemble, and make sure it is suitable for your balcony's wind and weather conditions. Additionally, think about the style and material of the structure to ensure it complements your existing decor.

Pros
  • Increased yield
  • Aesthetic appeal
  • Space efficiency
Cons
  • Higher cost
  • Assembly required
  • Wind vulnerability
Watch out

Be cautious not to overload your trellis or arbor with too many plants, as this can lead to structural damage and reduced plant health

FAQ

What type of plants are best suited for trellises?

Climbing plants like peas, beans, and tomatoes thrive on trellises

How do I secure my trellis to the balcony?

Use sturdy brackets or screws to anchor your trellis to the balcony railing or wall

Can I use a trellis for non-climbing plants?

Yes, you can use a trellis to provide support for top-heavy plants or to create a decorative screen
