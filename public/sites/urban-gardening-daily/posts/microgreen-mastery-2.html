Growing Microgreens in Small Spaces

Unlock year-round harvests

Takeaway

Microgreens can be grown indoors with minimal equipment and care

What happened

Microgreens have gained popularity in recent years due to their high nutritional value and ease of growth. They can be harvested in as little as 1-3 weeks and can be grown on a windowsill or under LED lights. This makes them an ideal crop for apartment dwellers and balcony gardeners with limited space.

Why it matters

Microgreens provide a continuous supply of fresh greens, even in the dead of winter, and can add a burst of flavor to a variety of dishes. They also require minimal space and care, making them a great option for those new to gardening. By growing microgreens, urban gardeners can enjoy a constant harvest and experiment with new flavors and recipes.

How to think about it

When growing microgreens, it's essential to use a good quality growing medium and to keep the soil consistently moist. Provide adequate light, and harvest the greens when they are 1-3 inches tall. With a little practice, you can master the art of growing microgreens and enjoy a year-round supply of fresh, delicious greens.

Pros
  • Year-round harvest
  • Minimal space required
  • High nutritional value
Cons
  • Requires consistent moisture
  • Can be sensitive to light
  • Limited crop selection
Watch out

Be cautious not to overwater, as this can lead to mold and poor germination

FAQ

What are microgreens?

Young, nutrient-dense versions of herbs and greens

How much light do microgreens need?

Most microgreens require 12-16 hours of light per day

Can microgreens be grown outdoors?

Yes, but they prefer partial shade and consistent moisture
