Tactile Switch Trends 2024

A quick look at the tactile switches gaining traction this year.

Takeaway

New tactile designs focus on refined feel without sacrificing durability.

What happened

Several manufacturers released updated tactile stems with smoother sliders and modest actuation forces. The market saw a surge in limited‑run variants featuring matte finishes.

Why it matters

Builders seeking a distinct thock benefit from these refinements, while retailers get fresh inventory to attract enthusiasts.

How to think about it

When choosing a tactile, weigh actuation force, housing material, and sound profile. Test a few samples before committing to a bulk purchase.

Pros
  • Improved smoothness
  • Consistent actuation
  • Modern aesthetics
Cons
  • Higher price than basic models
  • Limited availability
  • Potential compatibility issues with older PCBs
Watch out

Stock can disappear quickly during launch windows.

FAQ

Do newer tactile switches require different lubing?

They generally accept the same lubes, but thinner formulations work better with tighter tolerances.

Are they compatible with standard MX plates?

Yes, they follow the MX cross‑compatible dimensions.

Will they affect typing speed?

Feel changes may require a short adjustment period, but speed impact is minimal.
