Trim Your Prop Wash for Cleaner Flights

A quick guide to shaping props and motor mounts to cut turbulence and protect your camera.

Takeaway

Fine‑tuning prop wash reduces visual distortion and improves control without major redesign.

What happened

Pilots noticed streaks and wobble when flying close to obstacles. By adding small fairings and adjusting prop pitch, the airflow became smoother. The changes were tested on a standard 5‑inch quad.

Why it matters

Cleaner airflow means the camera sees less vibration, leading to steadier footage. It also eases the workload on the flight controller during aggressive maneuvers. The benefit is immediate and low‑cost.

How to think about it

Start with a thin carbon fairing behind each motor and test a few pitch angles. Keep the added weight under a gram per arm. Observe the video feed for reduced wobble before finalizing.

Pros
  • Improved video stability
  • Minimal weight increase
  • Easy to install
Cons
  • May slightly affect thrust
  • Requires test flights
  • Adds a small cost
Watch out

Avoid covering too much of the prop area, as it can cause overheating.

FAQ

Will this affect my thrust?

A small fairing may reduce peak thrust slightly, but the trade‑off is usually worth the smoother flight.

Do I need a new prop?

No, you can keep your existing prop set and just adjust the mount.

Can I use any material?

Light carbon fiber or thin PETG works best for low weight.
