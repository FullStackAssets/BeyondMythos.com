Cool Your Batteries to Tame Prop Wash Heat

Integrating passive cooling fins on the battery can reduce heat that amplifies prop wash turbulence.

Takeaway

A cooler battery maintains consistent voltage, keeping prop wash effects predictable.

What happened

Pilots reported that batteries heating up during long indoor sessions caused motor jitter. Adding thin aluminum fins to the battery case dissipated heat. Flight tests showed steadier throttle response.

Why it matters

Temperature spikes affect motor KV performance, which can worsen airflow irregularities. Maintaining battery temperature improves overall flight consistency. The solution is inexpensive and reversible.

How to think about it

Attach lightweight aluminum or copper fins using double‑sided tape on the battery's sides. Ensure they don't interfere with prop clearance. Monitor temperature with a simple thermal pad during a test flight.

Pros
  • Better voltage stability
  • Low cost
  • Easy to add/remove
Cons
  • Adds minor drag if protruding
  • May affect battery mounting
  • Needs periodic cleaning
Watch out

Make sure the fins don't short any battery contacts.

FAQ

Will this add much weight?

A few grams per side, negligible for most builds.

Can I use plastic fins?

Metal conducts heat better, but high‑temperature plastics can work.

Do I need to re‑balance the drone?

Check balance after installation; minor shifts may require adjustment.
