Cleaning Tiny Brushes: A No‑Nonsense Routine

Keep your micro brushes sharp with a fast, reliable cleaning method.

Takeaway

A quick rinse followed by gentle soap keeps bristles from swelling.

What happened

We cleaned three different 0.5 mm brushes after a day of painting, using lukewarm water, mild soap, and a soft cloth. Each brush retained its point shape and felt ready for the next session.

Why it matters

Dirty bristles cause uneven strokes and can embed dried paint, leading to premature wear and loss of detail.

How to think about it

Rinse immediately after use, work a drop of gentle soap through the tip, then pat dry on a paper towel. Store upright to let excess moisture drain.

Pros
  • Fast turnaround
  • Preserves tip integrity
  • Uses common household items
Cons
  • Requires immediate attention
  • Soap residue can affect paint if not rinsed fully
  • May not remove heavy pigment buildup
Watch out

Never use harsh solvents on synthetic fibers; they can dissolve the bristles.

FAQ

Can I use brush cleaners?

A mild brush cleaner works, but plain soap is sufficient for most acrylics.

How often should I deep‑clean?

A thorough soak once a month prevents stubborn buildup.

Is air‑drying safe?

Yes, as long as the brush is stored tip‑up to avoid water pooling in the ferrule.
