Preserve the Past for the Future

Protect local history for generations to come

Takeaway

Digital preservation is crucial for local history

What happened

Digital preservation is a vital part of any archive walk, as it helps to protect and conserve local history for future generations. By digitizing archives and historical documents, you can ensure that they remain accessible and intact, even as physical copies deteriorate or are lost. From scanning to cloud storage, every step in the digital preservation process is crucial to safeguarding local heritage.

Why it matters

Digital preservation is essential for local history, as it helps to prevent the loss of valuable information and cultural heritage. By preserving archives and historical documents digitally, you can promote their accessibility and usability, and ensure that they remain a vital part of your community's identity and heritage.

How to think about it

To get the most out of digital preservation, start by assessing the condition and significance of the archives and documents you want to preserve. Use high-quality scanning and digitization techniques to create accurate and durable digital copies, and store them securely using cloud storage or other digital repositories. Consider collaborating with experts and institutions to ensure the long-term preservation and accessibility of your digital archives.

Pros
  • Increased accessibility
  • Improved preservation
  • Cost-effective
Cons
  • Technical challenges
  • Limited resources
  • Dependence on technology
Watch out

Be aware of digital preservation standards and best practices

FAQ

What is digital preservation?

The process of converting physical archives to digital formats

How can I get started with digital preservation?

Assess your archives, invest in scanning equipment, and use cloud storage

Can I collaborate with institutions?

Yes, through partnerships, grants, and expert guidance
