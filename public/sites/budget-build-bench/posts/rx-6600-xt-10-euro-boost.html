RX 6600 XT: squeezing performance for under €150

A deep dive into the RX 6600 XT’s 10‑euro price drop and what it means for budget 1080p builds.

Takeaway

The RX 6600 XT now delivers solid 1080p gaming at a price that rivals entry‑level cards.

What happened

AMD slashed the MSRP of the RX 6600 XT by €10 after a supply glut, bringing the card down to €149. Retailers quickly adjusted, and we grabbed a unit for testing.

Why it matters

For budget builders, the price cut makes a mid‑range GPU accessible without sacrificing 1080p frame rates. It also pressures Nvidia’s low‑end lineup, tightening the value proposition across the board.

How to think about it

If you’re hunting a 1080p gaming rig under €500, prioritize the RX 6600 XT over older 1650‑Super cards, but pair it with 8 GB of fast RAM to avoid bottlenecks. Keep an eye on power draw; a 450 W PSU is sufficient. And don’t forget to enable Smart Access Memory for an extra 5‑10 % boost.

Pros
  • Strong 1080p performance
  • Low power draw
  • Now sub‑€150
Cons
  • Limited 1440p headroom
  • VRAM capped at 8 GB
  • Availability still spotty
Watch out

Watch out for counterfeit refurbished units that still list the old MSRP.

FAQ

Is the 10‑euro price cut enough to beat the GTX 1660 Super?

In most 1080p titles, the RX 6600 XT now edges out the GTX 1660 Super by 10‑15 % while costing less.

Do I need a newer motherboard for Smart Access Memory?

Only if you have a Ryzen 5000 series CPU and a compatible B55/X570 board; otherwise, it defaults to standard PCIe performance.

Can I overclock the RX 6600 XT safely on a budget build?

A modest 5‑10 % boost is safe with a decent aftermarket cooler, but large overclocks will push power draw past the 450 W recommendation.
