Pour-over Perfection

Tips for a flawless cup.

Takeaway

Mastering pour-over technique can significantly improve your coffee's flavor.

What happened

We explored the art of pour-over brewing, from equipment selection to water temperature. The key to a perfect cup lies in the nuances of technique, including pour rate and coffee-to-water ratio. Experimenting with different roasts and grind sizes also yielded interesting results.

Why it matters

Pour-over brewing allows for a high degree of control over the brewing process, making it an ideal method for coffee enthusiasts who want to tailor their cup to their taste preferences. By mastering pour-over technique, you can bring out the unique characteristics of your favorite coffee beans.

How to think about it

When approaching pour-over brewing, consider the importance of using fresh, high-quality coffee beans and filtered water. Invest in a good grinder and scale to ensure consistency. Practice makes perfect, so don't be discouraged if your first few attempts don't yield the desired results.

Pros
  • Flexible
  • Cost-effective
  • Flavorful
Cons

  • Manual
  • Sensitive
Watch out

Be mindful of water temperature to avoid under or over extraction.

FAQ

What's the ideal water temperature for pour-over?

Between 195°F and 205°F for optimal extraction.

How do I prevent channeling in pour-over?

Use a consistent pour rate and ensure the coffee bed is evenly saturated.

Can I use a pour-over dripper with any coffee filter?

No, use a filter specifically designed for pour-over to prevent paper taste and ensure clean extraction.
