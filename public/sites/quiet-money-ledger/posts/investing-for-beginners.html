Investing 101

Start investing today

Takeaway

Investing is a long-term game

What happened

Investing can seem intimidating, especially for beginners. However, it's an important step in building wealth and securing your financial future. By starting to invest early, you can take advantage of compound interest and potentially earn higher returns over time.

Why it matters

Investing can help you achieve your long-term financial goals, such as retirement or buying a house. It can also provide a hedge against inflation and help you build wealth over time.

How to think about it

Consider starting with a low-cost index fund or ETF, and gradually increasing your investment amount over time. Make sure to educate yourself on the basics of investing and avoid getting caught up in get-rich-quick schemes.

Pros
  • Can provide higher returns
  • Helps build wealth
  • Diversifies portfolio
Cons
  • Involves risk
  • Requires patience
  • Can be complex
Watch out

Don't invest more than you can afford to lose

FAQ

What is investing?

Putting money into assets to earn returns

How do I get started with investing?

Consider a brokerage account or a robo-advisor

What are the risks of investing?

There is a risk of losing some or all of your investment
