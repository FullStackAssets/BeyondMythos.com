Credit Score 101

Understand your credit score

Takeaway

Check your credit report regularly

What happened

Your credit score is an important factor in determining your creditworthiness and can affect your ability to get loans or credit. By understanding how credit scores work and taking steps to improve your credit, you can potentially save money on interest rates and achieve your financial goals.

Why it matters

A good credit score can help you qualify for lower interest rates and better loan terms, while a poor credit score can limit your access to credit and increase your costs.

How to think about it

Consider checking your credit report regularly to ensure it's accurate and up-to-date. Make sure to pay your bills on time and keep your credit utilization ratio low to maintain a healthy credit score.

Pros
  • Helps qualify for loans
  • Can lower interest rates
  • Improves financial health
Cons
  • Can be affected by errors
  • May not be entirely accurate
  • Requires maintenance
Watch out

Don't ignore errors on your credit report

FAQ

What is a credit score?

A number representing creditworthiness

How is my credit score calculated?

Based on payment history, credit utilization, and other factors

How can I improve my credit score?

Pay bills on time, keep credit utilization low, and monitor your credit report
