Debt Consolidation 101

Simplify your debt

Takeaway

Combine debts into one loan

What happened

Debt can be overwhelming, especially when you have multiple loans with different interest rates and payment due dates. Debt consolidation can help simplify your finances and potentially save you money. By combining your debts into one loan, you can make a single monthly payment and potentially lower your interest rate.

Why it matters

Debt consolidation can help you get out of debt faster and save money on interest. It can also help reduce stress and make managing your finances easier.

How to think about it

Consider consolidating your debt into a single loan with a lower interest rate and a longer repayment period. Make sure to read the fine print and understand the terms of the new loan.

Pros
  • Simplifies finances
  • Can lower interest rate
  • Reduces stress
Cons
  • May not address underlying issues
  • Can be expensive
  • Requires discipline
Watch out

Don't consolidate debt into a loan with a higher interest rate

FAQ

What is debt consolidation?

Combining multiple debts into one loan

How do I consolidate my debt?

Consider a balance transfer credit card or a personal loan

Will debt consolidation hurt my credit score?

It may temporarily lower your credit score, but can help in the long run
