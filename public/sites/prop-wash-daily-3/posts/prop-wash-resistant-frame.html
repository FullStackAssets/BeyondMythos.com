Surviving Prop Wash: A Frame Built for the Turbulence

A new 5‑inch frame design tackles the nasty prop wash that can shake loose components on aggressive pilots.

Takeaway

Designing with stiffer mounts and sealed electronics can keep your drone intact during heavy prop wash.

What happened

We tested a prototype frame with reinforced motor mounts and a sealed flight controller housing in a high‑speed indoor course. The frame withstood repeated hard hits from prop wash without any loosening of screws.

Why it matters

Prop wash can cause micro‑vibrations that lead to signal loss or component fatigue, especially for freestyle pilots who push the limits. A robust frame reduces maintenance downtime and extends the life of expensive parts.

How to think about it

When building, prioritize mounting rigidity and protect sensitive boards with silicone gaskets. Use slightly larger standoffs to give room for airflow but keep the center of gravity low. Check for any loose hardware after each intense flight session.

Pros
  • Stiff motor mounts
  • Sealed flight controller
  • Easy to assemble
Cons
  • Slight weight increase
  • Higher material cost
  • Limited color options
Watch out

Don’t over‑tighten the screws or you may crack the carbon fiber.

FAQ

Can I retrofit the design onto my existing frame?

Yes, the motor mounts are compatible with standard 5‑inch frames, though you’ll need to replace the top plate.

Will the sealed housing affect heat dissipation?

It adds a small thermal barrier, so ensure you have adequate airflow or use a low‑profile heat sink.

Is the frame compatible with 4‑cell LiPo setups?

The design accommodates typical 4‑cell layouts, but verify clearance for your specific battery shape.
