Puppy‑Proof Your Home in One Weekend

Quick fixes to keep curious puppies safe and your floors intact.

Takeaway

A few simple steps can prevent most common puppy mishaps.

What happened

First‑time owners often discover chewed cords and tipped plants within days. Simple barriers and safe zones stop the chaos before it starts.

Why it matters

Preventing accidents protects your puppy’s health and saves you repair costs. Early safety habits also teach boundaries.

How to think about it

Identify hazards, use baby gates, cover outlets, and store toxins out of reach. Keep a puppy‑safe kit handy for quick fixes.

Pros
  • Reduces injuries
  • Saves money
  • Builds routine
Cons
  • Initial time investment
  • May need temporary furniture rearrangement
  • Can be overkill for very short‑term puppies
Watch out

Don’t block all airflow when sealing off rooms; puppies still need ventilation.

FAQ

Do I need to puppy‑proof every room?

Focus on high‑traffic areas and places where you keep food or chemicals.

Can I use furniture covers as barriers?

Yes, slipcovers and chew‑proof blankets work well for temporary protection.

How long should I keep the gates up?

Until the puppy reliably responds to commands and avoids restricted zones.
