Tire Pressure Quick Check

A simple routine keeps rolling resistance low and puncture risk down.

Takeaway

Check pressure before each ride to maintain optimal performance.

What happened

A rider felt a sluggish start on a flat‑topped road and discovered under‑inflated tires after a quick gauge check. Re‑inflating restored smooth rolling.

Why it matters

Proper pressure improves efficiency, handling, and reduces pinch flats on rough urban surfaces. Consistency also extends tire life.

How to think about it

Carry a compact hand pump or CO₂ cartridge, and learn the recommended pressure range for your tire width. Check pressure at the start and after long rides.

Pros
  • Better efficiency
  • Fewer flats
  • Longer tire life
Cons
  • Carry pump
  • Need gauge
  • Pressure drops over time
Watch out

Avoid over‑inflating; a tire that feels hard may reduce grip on wet pavement.

FAQ

How often does pressure change?

It can drop a few PSI over a day, especially in temperature shifts.

Can I use a floor pump at work?

Yes, if you have space; a portable pump works anywhere.

What’s a safe pressure for city tires?

Follow the range printed on the sidewall, typically 50‑70 psi for narrow road tires.
