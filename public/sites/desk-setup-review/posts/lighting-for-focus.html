Shine a Light on Productivity

Get the right light for your work

Takeaway

Proper lighting can significantly impact focus and productivity

What happened

The importance of proper lighting in the workplace has long been overlooked, but recent studies have highlighted its significant impact on productivity and overall well-being. With the rise of remote work, creating a well-lit workspace has become a top priority for many workers. From natural light to artificial lighting solutions, there are many options available to create an optimal lighting environment.

Why it matters

Lighting can greatly affect our mood, energy, and ability to focus. By investing in a well-lit workspace, remote workers can improve their productivity, reduce eye strain, and create a more comfortable work environment. Whether you work from home or in a co-working space, proper lighting is essential for achieving your goals and maintaining a healthy work-life balance.

How to think about it

When it comes to lighting, think about the specific needs of your workspace. Consider the type of work you do, the time of day, and the natural light available. Look for lighting solutions that are adjustable, energy-efficient, and tailored to your specific needs. By taking the time to research and invest in proper lighting, you can create a workspace that supports your productivity and well-being.

Pros
  • Improved focus
  • Enhanced mood
  • Reduced eye strain
Cons
  • Expensive solutions
  • Limited natural light
  • Overwhelming options
Watch out

Be mindful of harsh or dim lighting, which can negatively impact productivity

FAQ

What type of lighting is best for productivity?

Natural light is ideal, but high-quality artificial lighting can also be effective

How can I reduce eye strain from screen time?

Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look away from your screen and focus on something 20 feet away for 20 seconds

Can I use string lights in my workspace?

Yes, but be mindful of the color temperature and brightness to avoid distractions
