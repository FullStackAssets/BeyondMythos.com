Lubing Trends: What’s Hot in 2024

Lubing techniques evolve, with new greases and tools shaping smoother keystrokes across tactile and linear switches.

Takeaway

Modern lubes and precision applicators make consistent results more reachable.

What happened

Recent forum threads highlight a shift toward thin synthetic greases for tactile switches and thicker oils for linear types. Builders report noticeable reduction in scratch and a quieter bottom‑out.

Why it matters

Consistent lubing improves switch feel, reduces wear, and can lower noise—key factors for both typing and gaming environments. Choosing the right viscosity prevents over‑lubing, which can dull the tactile bump.

How to think about it

Start with a small amount of lube on a brush, apply to the spring and housing interior, and test each switch before full assembly. Keep a clean workspace to avoid contaminating the lube.

Pros
  • Smoother keystroke
  • Reduced noise
  • Longer switch life
Cons
  • Time‑intensive process
  • Risk of over‑lubing
  • Requires practice with tools
Watch out

Using petroleum‑based lubes on POM stems can cause discoloration over time.

FAQ

Do I need a lube gun for keyboard switches?

A brush is sufficient for most DIY lubing tasks.

Is lubing necessary for clicky switches?

Lubing can quiet the bottom‑out but may reduce the click feel.

Can I reuse leftover lube?

Store it in a sealed container to maintain consistency.
