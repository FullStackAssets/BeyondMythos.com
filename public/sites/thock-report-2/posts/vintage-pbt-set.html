Vintage PBT Set Takes the Spotlight

A retro‑inspired PBT set resurfaces, offering deep‑dye colors and a matte texture that resists shine after months of use.

Takeaway

The set proves that classic materials still win in durability and feel.

What happened

The set was released through a limited run, featuring double‑shot legends and a muted color palette reminiscent of early 2000s keyboards. Community unboxings highlighted the consistent texture across all rows.

Why it matters

PBT’s resistance to shine keeps the keys looking fresh, while double‑shot legends prevent fading. For builders, the set offers a reliable aesthetic without frequent replacement.

How to think about it

Pair the set with a matte‑finished case to maintain a cohesive look, and consider keycap profile (e.g., OEM vs SA) for ergonomics. Verify the set includes the necessary sizes for your layout.

Pros
  • No shine over time
  • Double‑shot legends
  • Rich color palette
Cons
  • Limited artisan options
  • Higher cost than ABS
  • May feel chunky on small boards
Watch out

Some caps have a tighter fit on certain stems, so test a few before a full swap.

FAQ

Are the caps compatible with Cherry MX stems?

Yes, they follow the MX cross‑stem design.

Do the legends wear off with heavy use?

The double‑shot process makes them highly resistant to wear.

Is the set available in different profiles?

Only the OEM profile is offered in this release.
