Leica M6: The Gold Standard of Rangefinders?

Leica M6 reviewed for its build and shooting experience.

Takeaway

The Leica M6 is a masterpiece of mechanical engineering with unparalleled build quality.

What happened

The Leica M6 is a mechanical rangefinder that has been a benchmark for photographers. Its durability and precision mechanics make it a favorite among professionals and enthusiasts alike. Introduced in the 80s, it remains highly sought after today.

Why it matters

For film photographers, the camera is an extension of their creative vision. The Leica M6, with its compact size and lens quality, offers an unmatched shooting experience. Its metering system, although basic, is reliable and easy to use.

How to think about it

When considering the Leica M6, think about your shooting style. Its manual focus and aperture priority mode require a thoughtful approach. Practice will make you proficient, and the camera will reward you with exceptional images.

Pros
  • Exceptional build quality
  • Compact and portable
  • Outstanding lens quality
Cons
  • Manual focus can be challenging
  • No autofocus
  • Expensive
Watch out

Be aware that the Leica M6 requires a significant investment, both in terms of money and time to master its use.

FAQ

Is the Leica M6 worth the investment?

For serious film photographers, yes, due to its exceptional build and image quality.

Can I use the Leica M6 with digital lenses?

No, it's designed for use with M-mount film lenses.

How durable is the Leica M6?

It's known for its robust construction and can last for decades with proper care.
