Navigation Nightmares: Avoiding Common Mistakes

Stay safe on the water with a solid plan

Takeaway

A well-planned route can be the difference between a successful trip and a disaster

What happened

Many kayakers and paddlers have found themselves lost or stranded due to poor planning. This can happen to even the most experienced paddlers, and it's often caused by a lack of preparation or a failure to adapt to changing conditions. By taking the time to plan your route and prepare for emergencies, you can minimize the risk of getting lost or injured.

Why it matters

Navigation is a critical aspect of kayaking and paddling, and it's essential to get it right. A good plan can help you avoid hazards, find the best routes, and make the most of your time on the water. By developing strong navigation skills, you can become a more confident and self-sufficient paddler, and you'll be better equipped to handle any situation that arises.

How to think about it

To paddle with a plan, start by researching your route and checking the weather forecast. Make sure you have the necessary skills and equipment, and consider paddling with a buddy or joining a guided tour. It's also essential to stay flexible and be prepared to adapt your plan if conditions change or you encounter unexpected obstacles.

Pros
  • Increased safety
  • Improved navigation
  • Enhanced confidence
Cons
  • Requires planning and preparation
  • Can be time-consuming
  • May limit spontaneity
Watch out

Don't rely solely on technology, as GPS devices and other tools can fail or run out of battery

FAQ

What's the best way to plan a kayaking route?

Use a combination of maps, guides, and online resources to research your route and plan your trip

How can I stay safe while paddling alone?

Let someone know your itinerary and expected return time, and consider carrying a personal locator beacon (PLB) or satellite phone

What should I do if I get lost while kayaking?

Stay calm, try to find a landmark or reference point, and use your navigation skills to find your way back to your planned route
