Using a Quiet Money Ledger for Debt Snowball

Turn your ledger into a debt‑payoff engine with the classic snowball method.

Takeaway

Prioritizing smallest balances accelerates progress and motivation.

What happened

You listed each debt, its interest rate, and minimum payment. You then allocated extra cash to the smallest balance while maintaining other minimums.

Why it matters

Clearing debts one by one creates visible wins, which fuels continued saving. It also reduces the total interest you’ll pay over time.

How to think about it

Update the ledger each time a payment clears a balance, then roll the freed amount into the next smallest debt. Keep the ledger tidy to avoid missed payments.

Pros
  • Psychological boost
  • Simple to track
  • Flexible allocation
Cons
  • Ignores interest hierarchy
  • May extend payoff time
  • Requires regular updates
Watch out

Don’t neglect higher‑interest debt just because it’s larger.

FAQ

What if I can’t pay extra?

Even a modest extra amount speeds the snowball.

Should I include credit cards?

Yes—treat each card as a separate debt line.

Is the snowball better than avalanche?

Snowball favors motivation; avalanche focuses on interest savings.
