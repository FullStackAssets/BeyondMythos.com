Dialing In Your Home Espresso Shot Timing

Master the timing of grind, dose, and extraction for consistent, café‑quality espresso at home.

Takeaway

Precise timing is the hidden lever that transforms good espresso into great espresso.

What happened

I logged the seconds between grinding, tamping, and pulling a shot over a week, tweaking each interval. The data showed a sweet spot where flavor peaked.

Why it matters

Even with perfect grind size and pressure, a mistimed shot can under- or over-extract, muting nuance. Timing aligns chemistry with flavor.

How to think about it

Treat each step as a timed block: grind for 5‑7 seconds, tamp within 2 seconds, start extraction 3 seconds after the pump clicks. Use a timer or smartphone app to keep consistency.

Pros
  • Sharper flavor balance
  • Repeatable results
  • Easier troubleshooting
Cons
  • Adds a step to workflow
  • Requires a timer
  • May feel mechanical
Watch out

Don’t obsess over milliseconds; aim for consistent seconds to stay enjoyable.

FAQ

Do I need a fancy timer?

A phone stopwatch works fine; just keep it visible.

What if my machine has a pre‑infusion delay?

Start counting after the pump pressure stabilizes, treating pre‑infusion as part of the extraction window.

Can timing replace grind adjustments?

No, timing complements grind size—both must be tuned together.
