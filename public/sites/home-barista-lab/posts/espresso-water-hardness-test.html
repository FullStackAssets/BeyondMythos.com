Check Water Hardness with a Simple Test Strip

A quick strip test reveals if your tap water is too hard, helping you fine‑tune flavor without pricey equipment.

Takeaway

Know your water hardness to protect equipment and extract cleaner espresso.

What happened

I noticed a lingering metallic taste and faster scale buildup on my group head. A cheap test strip showed my water was borderline hard. After a simple filter swap, the taste brightened and cleaning intervals lengthened.

Why it matters

Hard water deposits alter temperature stability and restrict flow, compromising shot consistency. Softer water preserves grinder burrs and prolongs boiler life, making each brew more reliable.

How to think about it

Treat water as a variable like dose or grind size; test it monthly and adjust with a filter or mineral blend. If strips read above the recommended range, consider a reverse‑osmosis unit or a cartridge filter. Keep a log alongside your other espresso parameters.

Pros
  • Inexpensive
  • Fast results
  • No lab needed
Cons
  • Strip accuracy limited
  • Requires periodic testing
  • May need additional filtration
Watch out

Avoid over‑softening water, which can lead to flat extraction and under‑extracted flavors.

FAQ

How often should I test my water?

A monthly check is enough for most households, or after any plumbing changes.

Can I use the same strips for pour‑over?

Yes, water hardness affects any brewing method, so the test is universally useful.

Do I need a professional water analysis?

Not for home use; a strip gives a reliable range for everyday adjustments.
