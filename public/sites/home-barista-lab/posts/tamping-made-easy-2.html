Don't let tamping hold you back

Perfect espresso with less fuss

Takeaway

Use the right tamper to elevate your home espresso game

What happened

Tamping is often cited as a major hurdle for home baristas, with many struggling to achieve the perfect shot. Recently, we've seen a surge in tamper innovation, with new materials and designs entering the market. This shift has made it easier for enthusiasts to find a tamper that suits their needs.

Why it matters

A well-tamped shot is essential for optimal flavor extraction, and the right tamper can make all the difference. With the wrong tool, you may end up with channeling, under-extraction, or over-extraction, all of which can ruin an otherwise great shot. By choosing the right tamper, you can ensure a more consistent and delicious espresso experience.

How to think about it

When selecting a tamper, consider the size and shape of your portafilter, as well as your personal preference for material and ergonomics. Practice makes perfect, so don't be discouraged if it takes a few tries to get the hang of it. Start with a calibrated tamper and adjust as needed to find your sweet spot.

Pros
  • Improved flavor
  • Increased consistency
  • Reduced waste
Cons
  • Additional cost
  • Learning curve
  • Storage space
Watch out

Be cautious of low-quality tampers that may damage your equipment or compromise flavor

FAQ

What is the ideal tamper size?

The ideal tamper size matches your portafilter diameter

Can I use a tamper with a built-in level?

Yes, a built-in level can help ensure even tamping

How often should I clean my tamper?

Clean your tamper after each use to prevent old coffee oils from affecting flavor
