The Hidden Power of Pre-Infusion: Boosting Espresso Extraction

A quick dive into why a brief pre‑infusion can transform your shot’s body and flavor.

Takeaway

Add a 2‑second low‑pressure soak before full pressure for richer, more balanced espresso.

What happened

I set my machine’s pressure profile to 2 bar for 2 seconds, then ramped to 9 bar, and compared shots with and without the step. The pre‑infused shots showed fuller body and smoother acidity.

Why it matters

Pre‑infusion lets the coffee puck wet evenly, reducing channeling and unlocking nuanced flavors that standard ramps can miss.

How to think about it

Start with a modest 2‑second, 2‑bar soak; adjust time up to 4 seconds if your grind is fine or dose high, then return to your usual pressure.

Pros
  • Better balance
  • Reduced channeling
  • Easy machine setting
Cons
  • Requires pressure‑profile machine
  • Adds a few seconds per shot
  • May need grind tweak
Watch out

Too long or too high a pre‑infusion pressure can over‑extract and taste muddy.

FAQ

Will pre‑infusion work on a non‑PID machine?

You can simulate it with a manual pressure valve or by briefly pausing the pump, but precise control needs a PID or pressure‑profile machine.

How does pre‑infusion affect dose?

Generally keep your usual dose; if you notice bitterness, reduce dose slightly to compensate for the extra extraction time.

Can I use pre‑infusion with a bottomless portafilter?

Yes—watch the flow; a steady, slow bloom often produces a cleaner shot and reveals any channeling.
