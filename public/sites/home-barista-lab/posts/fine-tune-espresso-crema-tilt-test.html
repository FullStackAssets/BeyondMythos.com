Fine‑Tune Your Espresso Crema with a Simple Portafilter Tilt Test

A quick tilt test helps you spot uneven crema and improve shot consistency without pricey equipment.

Takeaway

A simple portafilter tilt reveals distribution issues so you can adjust grind or tamp for better crema.

What happened

I tried the tilt test on a recent batch of espresso and noticed the crema pooled to one side. Adjusting the tamp pressure and grinder burr alignment balanced the flow, producing a richer, even crema.

Why it matters

Uneven crema signals channeling, which wastes coffee and reduces flavor. Catching it early lets home baristas fine‑tune variables for consistent taste.

How to think about it

Place the portafilter on a flat surface, gently tilt it, and watch where the liquid gathers. If it runs to one edge, consider a firmer tamp or finer grind on that side. Small adjustments pay off in a balanced shot.

Pros
  • No extra tools
  • Immediate visual feedback
  • Improves shot consistency
Cons
  • Subjective interpretation
  • May miss subtle channeling
  • Requires steady hand
Watch out

Don’t over‑tilt the portafilter; a gentle angle is enough to see flow patterns.

FAQ

What if the tilt shows no obvious pooling?

Your extraction is likely even; focus on other variables like temperature or dose.

Can I use this test with a pressurized basket?

It’s less informative; the test works best with a non‑pressurized basket.

How often should I perform the tilt test?

Run it whenever you change beans, adjust grind, or notice inconsistent crema.
