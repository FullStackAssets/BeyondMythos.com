Refresh Your Tamper with a Simple Brush Routine

A quick brush-and‑wipe keeps your tamper clean, improving pressure consistency and taste.

Takeaway

Clean tampers deliver more even extraction and last longer.

What happened

I noticed uneven espresso shots after weeks of heavy use, traced to coffee oils coating my stainless steel tamper. A quick brush solved it.

Why it matters

Oil buildup changes the tamper’s friction, leading to variable pressure and subtle flavor shifts. Regular cleaning preserves consistency.

How to think about it

Treat your tamper like a kitchen tool: after each session, wipe with a dry brush, then a quick rinse if needed. Store it dry to avoid rust.

Pros
  • Improves pressure consistency
  • Extends tamper life
  • Easy and cheap
Cons
  • Takes a minute daily
  • Requires a dedicated brush
  • May need occasional deep cleaning
Watch out

Avoid abrasive pads that could scratch the tamper surface.

FAQ

How often should I clean my tamper?

A quick brush after every brew and a thorough wash weekly keeps it in top shape.

Can I use regular dish soap?

Mild soap is fine for a deep clean, but rinse well to remove residue.

What if my tamper is aluminum?

Use a non‑abrasive brush and avoid harsh chemicals to prevent discoloration.
