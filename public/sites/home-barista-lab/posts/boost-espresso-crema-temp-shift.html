Boost Your Espresso Crema with a Simple Temperature Shift

A quick temperature tweak can give your espresso a richer, silkier crema in minutes.

Takeaway

Raise brew temperature by 2 °C to thicken crema without over‑extracting.

What happened

I bumped my machine’s brew temp from 92 °C to 94 °C for a week, using the same beans and dose. The resulting shots showed noticeably fuller crema and a smoother mouthfeel. I logged extraction time and TDS to confirm balance stayed intact.

Why it matters

Crema is the visual cue of a well‑extracted espresso and a thicker layer often signals better aroma retention. Small temperature tweaks are a low‑cost way to elevate shot quality without new equipment.

How to think about it

Start with a stable baseline temp, then increase in 1 °C increments, tasting after each change. Watch for bitterness; stop when crema peaks and flavor stays sweet. Keep other variables constant so you isolate temperature’s effect.

Pros
  • Immediate visual improvement
  • No extra gear needed
  • Works with most home machines
Cons
  • Risk of over‑extraction if over‑done
  • May require recalibrating grinder dose
  • Not all machines allow fine temp control
Watch out

If your machine can’t hold the higher temperature, you may get temperature spikes that burn the coffee.

FAQ

Can I use this trick with a pressure‑stat machine?

Yes, just adjust the set‑point; the pressure profile stays the same.

Will raising temperature affect my espresso’s acidity?

A slight rise can mute bright acidity, so taste‑test to find your sweet spot.

Do I need to recalibrate my grinder after changing temperature?

Not necessarily, but a quick dose check ensures consistency.
