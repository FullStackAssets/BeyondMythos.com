Turn Your Phone Into an Espresso Shot Timer for Perfect Pulls

Use a free smartphone app to time your espresso pull and lock in consistent extraction without extra hardware.

Takeaway

A simple phone timer lets you nail shot times and improve consistency.

What happened

I noticed my shot times varied wildly, so I downloaded a basic timer app and started logging pull durations. Within a few days the numbers steadied and taste followed.

Why it matters

Shot time is a key variable that affects flavor balance; tracking it reveals over‑ or under‑extraction and guides adjustments.

How to think about it

Treat the timer as a feedback tool, not a strict rule—aim for your preferred window, note grind and dose changes, and tweak accordingly.

Pros
  • Zero cost
  • Instant feedback
  • Works with any machine
Cons
  • Phone may get splashed
  • Requires manual start/stop
  • No built‑in pressure data
Watch out

Keep the phone away from steam to avoid damage.

FAQ

Do I need a special app?

Any basic stopwatch or timer app works; choose one with lap function for easy logging.

What shot time should I target?

Typical espresso pulls range from 25 to 30 seconds, but adjust based on taste and bean.

Can I use this for other brew methods?

Yes, timing works for pour‑over, Aeropress, and even French press to improve consistency.
