Tame the Game Collection Beast

Get your games in order

Takeaway

A well-organized gaming shelf can save you time and reduce stress

What happened

A cluttered and disorganized gaming shelf can be overwhelming, making it difficult to find the games you want to play. A well-organized shelf, on the other hand, can save you time, reduce stress, and make your gaming experience more enjoyable. By categorizing your games, using storage solutions, and maintaining your shelf regularly, you can keep your game collection under control.

Why it matters

A well-organized gaming shelf matters because it provides a way to efficiently store and access your games. It's an excellent way to protect your games from damage, keep them clean, and prevent loss. A well-organized shelf also makes it easier to lend games to friends, trade games with others, and discover new games to play.

How to think about it

When organizing your gaming shelf, consider the type of games you have, the frequency of play, and the available space. Use storage solutions like shelves, bins, and boxes to keep your games categorized and easy to access. Label your games and storage solutions to make it easy to find what you're looking for. Don't be afraid to purge games that are no longer played or enjoyed.

Pros
  • Efficient storage
  • Easy access
  • Reduced stress
Cons
  • Time-consuming
  • Expensive
  • Limited space
Watch out

Be mindful of games that are sensitive to light, heat, or moisture when storing them

FAQ

What are some good storage solutions for games?

Consider shelves, bins, boxes, and file folders

How often should I organize my gaming shelf?

Regularly, ideally every 3-6 months

Can I store games in a basement or attic?

Yes, but be cautious of temperature, humidity, and pest control
