CRT Scanline Mod: Sharpen Your SNES Speedrun Vision

Add a cheap scanline overlay to your CRT for clearer timing cues and less eye strain during marathon runs.

Takeaway

A simple scanline mod can boost visual clarity and reaction time for competitive SNES speedrunning.

What happened

We sourced a thin transparent film printed with classic CRT scanlines and affixed it to the screen bezel of a vintage TV. After calibrating brightness, the overlay produced distinct horizontal bars without sacrificing overall image quality. The result was an instantly sharper view of sprite movement during practice runs.

Why it matters

Speedrunners rely on split‑second visual cues; even a slight blur can cost a frame. The added scanlines accentuate edges, making it easier to spot frame‑perfect inputs. For collectors who already own CRTs, the upgrade offers a performance edge without altering the original hardware.

How to think about it

Treat the mod as a visual aid, not a cheat—use it to reduce eye fatigue and improve consistency. Test with your favorite timer to ensure the overlay doesn’t shift perceived timing. Keep the film clean; dust can re‑introduce visual noise.

Pros
  • Low cost
  • Easy install
  • Improves frame perception
Cons
  • May dim brightness
  • Requires CRT
  • Potentially alters color fidelity
Watch out

Avoid covering the TV screen with opaque material that could cause overheating.

FAQ

Will the scanline film affect game colors?

It slightly mutates color intensity, but most runners find the trade‑off worth the sharper contrast.

Can I use this on modern LCDs?

The effect relies on phosphor‑based scanlines; LCDs don’t replicate the same visual benefit.

Is the overlay removable?

Yes—most films use static cling, so you can peel it off without residue.
