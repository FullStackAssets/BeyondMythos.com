Pixel Boost: Replacing Commodore 64 Power Supply Fuse for Reliable Boot

Swap the aging fuse in your C64’s PSU to eliminate random boot failures without breaking the bank.

Takeaway

A simple fuse swap can restore stable power to your C64 and keep your classics running.

What happened

We noticed a batch of C64 units powering up intermittently after years of use. Inspection revealed the stock 250mA fuse had corroded, causing voltage drops. Replacing it with a fresh 250mA glass fuse solved the issue.

Why it matters

Power instability can corrupt save files and damage cartridges, a nightmare for collectors. A reliable supply ensures your hardware preserves its value and your gameplay remains uninterrupted.

How to think about it

When a C64 refuses to boot or flickers, first check the fuse in the PSU. Use a multimeter to confirm continuity, then replace with the same rating glass fuse. Keep spare fuses on hand for future maintenance.

Pros
  • Cheap fix
  • Extends console lifespan
  • Easy DIY
Cons
  • Requires opening PSU
  • Potential voids warranty
  • Need proper fuse rating
Watch out

Ensure the replacement fuse matches the original rating to avoid overheating.

FAQ

How do I locate the fuse in a C64?

The fuse sits on the PSU board near the power connector; unscrew the PSU cover and look for a small glass cylinder.

Can I use a ceramic fuse instead?

Stick with the same type—glass—so the fuse blows safely; ceramic may behave differently.

Will this void my warranty?

Opening the PSU technically voids the original warranty, but many owners consider it worth the trade for reliability.
